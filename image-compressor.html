How to Compress an Image to 20KB or 50KB Online?

  1. Click on Select Image and choose your photo or signature from your mobile or PC.
  2. Adjust the Quality Slider (lower percentage gives smaller KB size).
  3. Click Compress Image Now to view the reduced file size instantly.
  4. Click Download Compressed Image to save it to your device.

Why Use Our Browser-Based Image Compressor?

Unlike other websites that upload your personal photos and ID signatures to external servers, QuickTools uses HTML5 Canvas technology to compress images directly inside your browser. This ensures 100% privacy and zero waiting time.
