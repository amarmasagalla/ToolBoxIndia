How to Compress an Image to Any Exact KB Size?

Online exam portals (SSC, UPSC, IBPS, TSPSC, APPSC) and passport/PAN card websites require strict file sizes such as 10KB to 20KB for signatures and 20KB to 50KB for photographs. With QuickTools.in, you don't need to guess compression percentages:

  1. Upload or drag-and-drop your JPG, PNG, or WebP file into the box above.
  2. Choose a quick preset (10KB, 20KB, 50KB, 100KB, 200KB, 500KB) or type any custom KB target.
  3. Click Compress Image Now—our smart algorithm automatically adjusts quality and dimensions to hit your target size.
  4. Download your ready-to-upload image immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are my personal photos uploaded to a server?
No. All compression happens 100% inside your own browser using HTML5 Canvas. Your files never leave your phone or computer.

Why did a PNG file compress to JPG?
PNG is a lossless format that cannot be compressed to tiny sizes like 20KB. Converting to JPEG or WebP allows extreme file size reduction while keeping text and faces sharp.
